02 · Pragmatic Urbanism Keep & Decorate

Destroy highways? NO. Decorate them with flowers & greenery.

Look to your left: what has already been built stays active with cars and trucks moving on top. We simply stop building new urban mega-highways, and wrap existing grey concrete in tropical vines, trees, and vibrant Malaysian flowers that cool the street and naturally absorb and disperse harsh traffic noise.

06 · 2036 · All Lines Converge 2036 · Public Office

2036: a Kuala Lumpur & Malaysia built for everyone.

Scroll just past this charter to take in the full, unobstructed 3D Kuala Lumpur skyline — map-accurate landmarks, sprawling neighborhoods, greened corridors, free-flowing RapidKL buses, and elevated rail.

PILLAR 01

Railways as the National Backbone

High-frequency LRT, MRT, reliable KTM Komuter, and intercity ETS rail connecting towns nationwide with seamless fare and walkway integration.

PILLAR 02

Enforced Bus Lanes Over New Highways

Halt urban mega-highway rubber-stamping. Physically enforce dedicated bus corridors like RapidKL 750 so buses beat private cars every morning.

PILLAR 03

Green Existing Concrete & Shaded Kaki Lima

Keep existing highways but wrap concrete pillars in tropical greenery, and build continuous tree-shaded sidewalks across Selangor and Malaysia.

PILLAR 04

Elderly-First Safe Crossings

Raised zebra crossings, generous pedestrian signal timings, and curb ramps so seniors, wheelchair users, and children cross without fear.

PILLAR 05

Vibrant Shared Spaces & Cycling

Protected bike lanes, colorful neighborhood shops, and safe public plazas where families take kids out and communities grow together.

PILLAR 06

Clean, Accountable Public Service

Zero tolerance for stealing public money. Open project tracking and leadership judged strictly by whether daily life improves on the street.
